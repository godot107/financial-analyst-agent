**Memo — MSFT gross margin percentage, latest 10-K**

**Answer in one line:** Gross margin percentage fell 0.9 pts to 67.9%, and the filing points to AI infrastructure investment and sales mix, partly offset by cloud efficiency gains [P30].

**The level and the move.** Company gross margin — gross profit over revenue — stands at 67.9% in fiscal 2026, against 68.8% in fiscal 2025 and 69.8% in fiscal 2024. Measured from fiscal 2024, the margin fell 1.8 pts to 67.9%. This is a comparison of Microsoft with its own prior years only; the data here contains no peer figures, so no competitor or industry benchmarking is possible, and no valuation multiple can be derived.

**What the filing says.** One passage states that gross margin percentage decreased, driven by continued investments in AI infrastructure and a sales mix shift to Azure, offset in part by efficiency gains in Azure [P30]. Consistent with that, Microsoft Cloud gross margin percentage is described as decreasing, driven by continued investments in AI infrastructure and growing AI product usage, offset in part by efficiency gains in Azure and Microsoft 365 Commercial cloud [P22]. A separate passage describes gross margin percentage increasing slightly, primarily driven by efficiency gains in Microsoft 365 Commercial cloud, offset in part by continued investments in AI infrastructure and growing AI product usage [P27]. The passages supplied do not identify which period each of these discussions covers, so the filing as excerpted here does not fully reconcile them.

**Below the gross line.** Net margin — net income over revenue — rose 4.2 pts to 40.3%, moving opposite to gross margin. The passages provided do not explain that divergence, so no cause is offered here.
